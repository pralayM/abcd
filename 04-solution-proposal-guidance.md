# Development Company Proposal Guidance
**Classification:** Confidential — For Recipients and Authorized Parties Only  
**Project:** AI Fraud Detection Platform with Blockchain Verification  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Issued for Company Review  
**Audience:** Development companies, solution partners, technical leads, product stakeholders, procurement, QA, security and operations  

*This document is a project/RFP planning document, not legal advice. Commercial and legal terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel before execution.*

## 1. Purpose

This document tells responding companies what information should be included in their proposal.

## 2. Requested proposal structure

### A. Company profile
- years in operation;
- relevant fintech experience;
- blockchain/Ethereum experience;
- security capabilities;
- comparable projects.

### B. Proposed solution
Explain:
- architecture;
- blockchain strategy;
- smart-contract approach;
- AI/risk integration;
- API/backend approach;
- data architecture;
- security model.

### C. Team
Provide:
- role;
- seniority;
- named resource where possible;
- location/time zone;
- allocation;
- relevant experience.

### D. Delivery plan
Provide:
- phases;
- milestones;
- dependencies;
- assumptions;
- estimated duration;
- acceptance approach.

### E. Security
Explain:
- key management;
- access control;
- secure development lifecycle;
- smart-contract review;
- vulnerability management;
- production deployment controls.

### F. Commercial proposal
Provide:
- fixed price and/or T&M;
- milestone costs;
- team rates;
- support/warranty;
- optional services;
- payment assumptions.

### G. Risks
Identify at least:
- blockchain provider dependency;
- data availability;
- model performance;
- smart-contract risks;
- scalability;
- regulatory/privacy considerations;
- delivery dependencies.

## 3. Technical proposal principle

We welcome improvements. Companies should explicitly identify where they recommend a change from the baseline and explain the benefit, cost and risk.
